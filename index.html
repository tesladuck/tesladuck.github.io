Straight answers

How do you keep to the budget?

Every project is priced as a fixed scope. Nothing outside it is built until you approve a written change in your portal, so the number you sign is the number you pay.

What if you find a problem behind a wall?

We inspect before we quote, and every older-home project carries a written contingency. If something turns up, you see photos, a plan and a price before work continues. Unused contingency is returned.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We prepare and submit permit applications, coordinate engineers and book inspections as part of the plan stage.

How soon can you start?

Site visits happen within the week. Start dates depend on permits and the schedule, and your quote lists a firm start date.

What does the client portal include?

A live schedule, daily photos, budget against quote, change approvals, documents and direct messages to your site lead, available around the clock.
